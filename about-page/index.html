The legend

According to legend, Icarus crafted wings of wax to escape his cage and attain freedom. He began to rise, soaring past his prison, but as he drew closer to the sun, its light dazzled his eyes. He climbed higher still, until the sun melted his waxen wings and he fell.

Most people read this story as a “warning”:

“Don’t fly too high, or you’ll fall.”

We read it differently.

Icarus may not have reached the sun, but he became the icon of those who possess a boundless passion to rise.

For us, the point is not the fall, it is the courage to rise in the first place.

We exist so that our business partners’ wings never melt.

As we carry you to the closest point to the sun (your goals), we offer you the sturdiest wings technology can provide.

Two Giants, One Roof

For years, there has been a persistent dilemma in the e-commerce world: either you chose the freedom and budget-friendly structure of WooCommerce and wrestled with its complexity, or you chose the quality and ease of Shopify and resigned yourself to high costs and a rental model.

We rejected this divide.

Brksoft exists to bring the best aspects of the world’s two largest e-commerce technologies together under a single roof.
