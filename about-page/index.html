An old story about wings and the sun is our compass. Here is how we read it.

Chapter I · The legend

Icarus and his father were locked in a tower by the sea. They built wings from feathers and wax, and one morning Icarus flew out over the walls. The higher he rose, the brighter the sun became, until its light dazzled him. He kept climbing. The wax melted, the feathers came loose, and he fell into the sea.

  1. wings of wax

  2. over the walls

  3. too close to the sun

  4. into the sea

For centuries, people have told it as a warning:

“Don’t fly too high, or you’ll fall.”

Chapter II · Another reading

We read it differently.

Icarus never reached the sun. Yet thousands of years later, his name still belongs to everyone who dares to rise.

We don’t remember the fall. We remember the courage it took to leave the ground.

Chapter III · Our promise

We build wings that never melt.

Your goals are your sun. Fly as close to them as you dare. We make sure your wings hold.

Chapter IV

Two Giants, One Roof

For years, opening an online store meant picking a side. WooCommerce gave you freedom and a fair price, along with plenty of complexity. Shopify gave you polish and ease, and a bill every month for a store you never really owned.

We refused to pick a side.

So we brought the best of both under one roof.

Epilogue
